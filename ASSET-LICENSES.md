# Asset licensing

## Weather artwork

The PNGs in `iweather@local/assets/weather-icons-figma` are exported from the
Weather Icons library supplied by the project owner:
[`Weather Icons in Figma`](https://www.figma.com/design/vDNQRZsP7L5gxEl0OAVauU/Untitled?node-id=3-199).
They are kept as local copies so the widget does not depend on short-lived Figma
asset URLs at runtime. Their redistribution terms should be confirmed with the
library owner before publishing the extension.

## Wallet artwork

The cryptocurrency artwork in `iweather@local/assets/wallet` is retained at the
request of the project owner. Before publishing, the owner should confirm that
the selected logos are allowed under the relevant project trademark and brand
guidelines.
